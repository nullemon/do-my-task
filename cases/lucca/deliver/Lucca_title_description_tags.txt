==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
Lucca was a U.S. Marine Corps specialized search dog, a German shepherd–Malinois mix trained to hunt for hidden explosives far out in front of her patrol. With her first handler, Gunnery Sgt. Chris Willingham, she served two tours in Iraq; with Cpl. Juan Rodriguez she led 75 patrols in Helmand Province, Afghanistan. Across more than 400 missions, no one was killed on the patrols she led.

On 23 March 2012 an explosive device went off beneath her. She survived but lost her front left leg, and was walking again within ten days. In 2016 she became the first U.S. Marine Corps dog to receive the PDSA Dickin Medal. This video uses real U.S. Marine Corps video of Lucca filmed in 2012.
