TabNap settings

Puts tabs you aren’t using to sleep so the browser frees their memory. A sleeping tab stays in your tab bar and reloads when you click it.

Automatic unloading

When off, TabNap leaves your tabs alone. “Unload other tabs now” still works.

Never unload these sites

A site also covers its subdomains: google.com covers mail.google.com, but mail.google.com doesn’t cover the rest of Google. Add a port to match only that port, like localhost:3000. Chrome’s Memory Saver and Edge’s sleeping tabs are told to leave these sites alone too.
