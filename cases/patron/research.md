## CONFIRMED FACTS

### Origin
- Jack Russell terrier, male. Born 20 July 2019 (his 3rd birthday was marked on 20 July 2022: Espreso; Wikipedia). NPR called him "2-year-old" in April and May 2022, which fits.
- His name means "cartridge" or "ammo" ("bullet" in some outlets). NPR: "ammo"; CBC: "bullet" or "cartridge". SAFE: "His name means 'cartridge' in Ukrainian."
- Owner and handler: **Mykhailo Iliev**, a pyrotechnic (bomb-disposal) specialist with the State Emergency Service of Ukraine (SES, Ukrainian abbreviation ДСНС, "DSNS") in Chernihiv region. Reuters and Espreso describe him as a **major of the Civil Protection Service**. UNICEF calls him head of the SES pyrotechnic works group in Chernihiv region. He has done this work since 2014 (NPR).
- How Patron came to the family: NPR says Iliev first bought him from a work colleague as a pet for his son. CBC quotes Iliev saying they wanted a compact breed that travelled well, with a pedigree, to raise as a show dog. UNICEF: Iliev and his wife **Iryna** (SES spokeswoman in Chernihiv region) "adopted a white and brown puppy".
- Before the war he was a family dog who did some training with a dog handler. After 24 Feb 2022 he finished specialised training (UNICEF). He was trained on the smell of gunpowder and got used to the sound of explosions (NPR; CBC).

### What he does
- When he smells explosives he signals (UNICEF: "he starts barking or just sits and patiently waits"), and the human team finds and removes the item (NPR; UNICEF).
- He weighs about 4 kg. CBC says most anti-personnel mines need at least 5 kg of pressure. SAFE wording: "He is so light that he is less likely to set off a pressure mine." Do NOT say he "cannot" trigger mines.
- His vest has a handle on top so Iliev can lift him away at once if there is a tripwire (CBC, Iliev quoted).
- He also teaches children mine safety. The SES slogan is "Do not approach! Do not touch! Call 101!" (UNICEF).
- His favourite treat is cheese (NPR, Iliev quoted: "Patron just loves cheese.").

### The medal (8 May 2022)
- On **Sunday 8 May 2022**, President **Volodymyr Zelensky** presented the awards at a joint news conference in Kyiv during the visit of Canadian Prime Minister **Justin Trudeau** (NPR; Reuters; LIGA; Espreso). Some outlets dated it 9 May, the day they published.
- **Patron** received the medal **"For Dedicated Service"** (Ukrainian: «За віддану службу») (LIGA; Espreso; NPR just says "a medal"). Zelensky handed it to Iliev.
- **Iliev** received the **Order for Courage, 3rd class** (Espreso; Reuters says the award "also went to" Iliev).
- Patron barked during the ceremony. Trudeau laughed and pretended to check his pockets for treats (NPR). The SES joked on Facebook that Patron enjoyed meeting Canada's leader, "even though Mr. Trudeau did not find a piece of Patron's favorite cheese" (NPR; quoted via Google translation, so label it as a translation if used).
- Zelensky quote (NPR, English): Patron is "a small but very famous sapper." In fuller form: "A dog who helps clean our land from the traces of the occupiers, and who also helps teach children mine safety." (from his remarks later that day). Use only these exact words, and label them as translated.

### Later honors and legacy (in date order)
- 28 Apr 2022: visited children at Okhmatdyt children's hospital in Kyiv for a safety lesson (SES photos on Commons; NPR mentions the hospital visit).
- 5 May 2022: became the mascot of the new International Coordination Center for Humanitarian Demining (Ministry of Internal Affairs emblem on Commons; Odessa Journal, via Wikipedia).
- **27 May 2022: Cannes Film Festival, Palm Dog "DogManitarian" award.** This was a special award; the main Palm Dog went to a poodle from *War Pony* (Hollywood Reporter; Fox/Storyful). He did not attend; a lookalike accepted it for him (Fox/Storyful, a single detail).
- June 2022: Golden Paw award from the Irish Kennel Club (SES photo caption on Commons only, so treat as WEAK and don't headline it).
- 12 Jul 2022: awards from US veterans' groups, Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW) and Bomb Techs Without Borders (SES photo captions on Commons).
- **1 Sep 2022: Ukrposhta charity stamp "Пес Патрон" ("Dog Patron").** Print run of 1 million sheets. Zelensky took part in the first-day cancellation at a school in Irpin, and Patron and Iliev attended (MVS; Ukrinform). It came second in a public vote, with almost 200,000 votes (MVS).
  - Proceeds: about **US$500,000**; 80% went to mine-clearing equipment, 20% to animal shelters (BBC, Dec 2024). On 14 Oct 2024 Ukrposhta handed the SES a demining machine bought with the stamp money (Ukrinform).
- 8 Sep 2022: US Secretary of State Antony Blinken met him at Okhmatdyt hospital (State Dept photos, public domain).
- 19 Nov 2022: UK PM Rishi Sunak met SES rescuers, with Patron, at a Kyiv fire station (Ministry of Internal Affairs photos).
- **20 Nov 2022: UNICEF Ukraine named him its first "Goodwill AmbassaDOG"**, a first in UNICEF's history (UNICEF press release; UNICEF story; CBC).
- Jan 2023: the animated mine-safety series about him launched online, made with UNICEF and supported by USAID (Wikipedia; Espreso). Production was halted in 2025, partway through season 2, after US cuts to USAID (RT/Pryamiy via Wikipedia; Cartoon Brew). Keep this brief and neutral if used.
- Mar 2023: first licensed comic, "Пес Патрон і Шкарпетковий монстр" ("Patron the Dog and the Sock Monster"), published in Kharkiv (Ukrinform).
- July 2023: met USAID Administrator Samantha Power at the SES in Kyiv (US Embassy Kyiv photo, public domain; its camera date wrongly reads 2022).
- 15 May 2024: featured in PBS *Nature*, "Saving the Animals of Ukraine" (via Wikipedia, not opened).
- 31 Dec 2024: false online reports said he had died. The SES posted: "Patron is perfectly fine. He is alive, healthy, continuing to perform his important duties…" (Ukrinform English translation).
- 14 Dec 2025: First Lady Olena Zelenska presented a children's book "from Patron" about emotions, «Коли ти…» ("When You…") (Ukrinform).
- 4 Feb 2026: NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte met Patron during a visit to Chernihiv region. Rutte mentioned him in a speech at the Munich Security Conference on 14 Feb 2026 (UNN; Euromaidan Press; Ukrainska Pravda). Paraphrase what Rutte said; translations of his remark differ.
- Sep 2026: "married" Lana, another Jack Russell (announced 2 Nov 2026 on his Instagram). Nov 2026: seven puppies (UNN; Mezha). Both outlets cite his own Instagram, so attribute it: "according to his official Instagram".
- Status as of Nov 2026: **alive**, about 7 years old, still a mascot and safety educator. NPR (2022) noted his Instagram had more than 220,000 followers; CBC (2024) put it at over 400,000.

## DISPUTED POINTS: safest wording
- **Service dog or mascot?** Iliev told LIGA (May 2022) that Patron is "not a service dog but a mascot" of the Chernihiv rescuers. SES photo captions call him a "landmine finder and mascot". SAFE: "a mine-detection dog and the mascot of Ukraine's State Emergency Service." Don't call him a "military dog" or an "army dog"; the SES is a civilian emergency service.
- **Counts** (see the table above). SAFE: "credited with helping find hundreds of explosive items", or "more than 300, according to UNICEF in 2023".
- **Medal name:** the dog's medal is "For Dedicated Service". Wikipedia says the Order for Courage went to both, but Ukrainian outlets say Iliev got the Order for Courage and Patron got the "For Dedicated Service" medal. SAFE: "Zelensky awarded Patron a medal, and his handler Mykhailo Iliev received the Order for Courage."
- **Name meaning:** "cartridge" (the most literal), "ammo" or "bullet". Use "cartridge".
- **Date of the medal:** 8 May 2022 (a Sunday), not 9 May.
- **Cannes:** he "received a special Palm Dog award", NOT "won the Palm Dog" or "won at Cannes".

## MYTHS AND POPULAR ERRORS to correct on screen
1. "Patron is in the Ukrainian army." He works with the civilian State Emergency Service.
2. "Patron found 200 mines by himself." He helps sniff them out; the human sappers locate and make the items safe. Counts are team finds credited to him.
3. "He's too light to ever set off a mine." Light weight lowers the risk; it doesn't remove it.
4. "Patron died" (viral hoax, Dec 2024). Denied by the SES.
5. "Patron won the Palm Dog at Cannes." He got a special humanitarian award.
6. "Zelensky pinned a medal on the dog." He handed it to Iliev.

## EMOTIONAL PEAK MOMENTS (all true)
1. The medal ceremony: in the middle of a war, a president and a prime minister stop at a news conference while a small terrier barks, and Trudeau jokes, patting his pockets for treats.
2. Okhmatdyt hospital, April 2022: Patron visits sick children and teaches them "Don't approach, don't touch, call 101."
3. The vest handle: his handler had a handle sewn on so he can scoop Patron up instantly if a tripwire is found (CBC).
4. The stamp: a postage stamp of a small dog raises about $500,000. Most of it buys a demining machine; the rest goes to animal shelters.
5. His third birthday: instead of a gift, his Instagram asked fans to help buy food for homeless cats and dogs, through the charity UAnimals (Espreso, July 2022).
6. 2026: he becomes a father of seven. Life goes on.
