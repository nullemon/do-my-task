==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
Bretagne (pronounced "Brittany") was a golden retriever trained by volunteer firefighter Denise Corliss for Texas Task Force 1. At just two years old, her very first deployment was Ground Zero after September 11, 2001, where she searched the rubble in 12-hour shifts and comforted exhausted rescue workers. She went on to search after Hurricanes Ivan, Rita and Katrina, then spent her retirement as a reading dog for children.

On June 6, 2016, as she made her last trip to the vet in Cypress, Texas, firefighters and search teammates lined the path and saluted. She is believed to be the last known search dog from Ground Zero. No free photos or video of Bretagne herself exist; the footage shows FEMA search-and-rescue dogs at work today and other golden retrievers, labelled "not Bretagne".

• "Last known" / "believed to be": every source hedges this, so we do too.
• The viral Ground Zero photos of a golden on a stretcher or on a pulley show other dogs (the pulley dog is Riley of Pennsylvania Task Force 1).
• She served with Texas Task Force 1, not Pennsylvania's team.
• Time at Ground Zero: about two weeks (sources say 10 days to two weeks). Retirement: around age nine.
