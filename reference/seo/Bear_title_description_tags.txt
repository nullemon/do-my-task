==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
Bear, an Australian koolie mix, was given up as a pet because of his obsessive energy. Trained by the University of the Sunshine Coast and sponsored by IFAW, he became a koala detection dog. During the 2019–2020 Black Summer bushfires he found more than 100 sick, injured and starving koalas, so they could be assessed and taken into care.
