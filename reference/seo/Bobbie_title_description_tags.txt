==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
In August 1923, Bobbie, a 2-year-old collie mix from Silverton, Oregon, was lost at a gas station in Wolcott, Indiana. Six months later, thin, matted and with his toenails worn to nothing, he walked back into his hometown. This is the true story of Bobbie the Wonder Dog, including real footage of Bobbie from the 1924 silent film he starred in.
