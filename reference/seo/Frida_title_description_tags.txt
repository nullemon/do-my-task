==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
Frida, a yellow Labrador in the Mexican Navy's canine unit, became a worldwide symbol of hope after the September 19, 2017 earthquake in Mexico City, searching collapsed buildings in her goggles and boots. Viral posts claimed she saved 52 lives; the navy's real figure is 12 people found alive and 43 found deceased over 55 operations. She retired in 2019 and died in 2022.
