==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
In January 1925, a diphtheria outbreak threatened the children of Nome, Alaska. The antitoxin had to travel 674 miles by dog sled relay. Balto ran the final 55 miles and became famous — but 12-year-old Togo, led by musher Leonhard Seppala, ran about 261 miles, including a dangerous crossing of the frozen Norton Sound in a -85°F wind chill.
