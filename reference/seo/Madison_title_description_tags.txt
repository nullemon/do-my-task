==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
When the Camp Fire destroyed Paradise, California in November 2018, Andrea Gaylord had minutes to escape and couldn't reach her dogs. Her Anatolian shepherd mix Madison survived, and for almost a month he guarded the ruins of his home, kept alive by a rescue volunteer who left food and water. In December, his family finally came back for him.
