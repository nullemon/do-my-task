==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
In April 2018, three-year-old Aurora wandered away from her family's farm near Warwick, Queensland, and spent more than 15 hours lost in rugged bushland overnight. Beside her the whole time was Max, the family's 17-year-old blue heeler, deaf and partly blind. In the morning, Max led her grandmother straight to her. Queensland Police made him an honorary police dog.
