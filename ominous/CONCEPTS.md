# OMINOUS — six original short-form videos

Series look: vertical 1080×1920, near-silent minimal typography (thin, widely letter-spaced, off-white text, lower third or centred), slow camera, film grain, soft vignette, desaturated palette with ONE accent colour per video. A low procedural drone soundtrack in every one. 20–30 s. Ending lands in the last 3 s and loops back to the first frame where possible. Every claim on screen is real science or real history; no fake news.

## 1. THE LAST SUNSET
A lone rock ridge silhouette on a barren world; sky full of stars. A year counter accelerates logarithmically: 10^10 → 10^14 years. The blue/white stars burn out first, the sky reddens and thins until a single dim red dwarf sets behind the ridge. Black. Last line: "After this, nothing ever shines again." Real: star formation ends ~10^14 yr; red dwarfs are the last stars (lifespans up to ~10^13 yr).

## 2. ROGUE
Deep space, Milky Way band. Something dark slowly occludes the stars from left — a black disc only visible by what it hides. Faint starlight reveals a frozen surface rim. Text beats: "No sun." / "No day." / "Billions may drift through our galaxy." / "You wouldn't see one coming." Real: microlensing surveys suggest free-floating planets may be numerous (estimates range up to billions or more in the Milky Way).

## 3. 72 SECONDS
Night, fog, a large radio dish on a ridge slowly tracking, red aviation light blinking. On-screen: a scrolling printout column; the characters 6EQUJ5 appear and get circled in red (the real "Wow!" signal printout style, recreated generically). Lines: "Aug 15, 1977." / "72 seconds." / "Never heard again." The dish keeps listening. Final: static hiss, a single blip… cut. Real: Big Ear telescope, Wow! signal, 72 s duration (Earth rotation sweep), never repeated.

## 4. LAST ONE ONLINE
Earth night side from low orbit, city lights as a procedural emissive texture. Aurora on the limb. Lights go out region by region in a slow wave, silent. Finally one single light remains blinking. Text: "Last one online." Then it goes too. Ambiguous, art piece; no factual claim needed (keep text poetic).

## 5. NO FLOOR
Camera descends through Jupiter's cloud decks: bright ammonia-ice cloud tops, then brownish layers, then darkness lit only by lightning flashes inside towering clouds. On-screen altimeter/pressure HUD: 1 bar → 10 → 100 → 1,000 bar; temperature climbing. Text: "No surface." / "Just pressure." Real: no solid surface; pressure rises continuously; lightning observed by Juno; ~1000s of bar deep inside.

## 6. ONE HOUR
A small craft hovering near a black hole: black disc with a glowing lensed accretion ring (procedural), stars distorted around it. Two clocks on screen: HERE and HOME. HERE ticks normally; HOME spins faster and faster as the ship descends, ending at "1 hour = 7 years". Final: the ship's light turns away. Real-ish: time dilation factor 61,000 (1 h ≈ 7 yr) requires a hover extremely close to a rapidly spinning black hole's horizon (Interstellar's Miller scenario) — say "near a spinning black hole".
