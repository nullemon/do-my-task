==================== YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION ====================
Patron is a Jack Russell terrier who works with the bomb-disposal team of Ukraine's State Emergency Service in the Chernihiv region, with his handler Mykhailo Iliev. He sniffs out explosives left behind in fields and villages so the human experts can make them safe. On May 8, 2022, during Justin Trudeau's visit, President Zelensky presented Patron with a medal for dedicated service.

He also teaches children mine safety, became UNICEF's first "Goodwill AmbassaDOG", and his charity stamp raised about $500,000. By early 2023, UNICEF said he had helped find more than 300 explosive objects. This video includes real footage of Patron at work and of the Ukrainian sapper teams he works with.

FACT-CHECK NOTES
• Counts are always "credited with" — finds are team finds; the latest reliable figure is "more than 300" (UNICEF, Jan 2023).
• He works with the civilian State Emergency Service, not the army.
• Being light lowers the risk of setting off a mine; it does not remove it.
• The December 2024 reports of his death were false (State Emergency Service).
• The medal was presented on May 8, 2022. The puppies (2026) are reported by his official Instagram.
